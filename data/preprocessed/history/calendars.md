# Calendars

---



The [[World of Aroka|world of Aroka]] has had many calendars spanning its cultures and ages. Remnants of long lost civilizations suggest that the movement of the Three Suns in [[The Void|the Void]] varied across the world's past. Key events, such as suns passing through the Voidgate or merging together in the sky, occurred at very different points in the year, making it difficult to approximate how long ago these calendars were in use. 

Throughout the early portions of the [[Age of Reclamation]], the emerging civilizations each had their own means of marking the beginning and end of the year. Most agreed that the natural cycle of seasons and movement of the Three Suns indicated that a year lasted 509 days, but exactly what to mark the new year varied significantly. Some would count the number of times one or more of the three suns passed through the Voidgate while others would wait for a particularly warm summer or cold winter to count a year completed.

This all changed when all Three Suns passed through the Voidgate, leaving the world in complete darkness even during the day. Even the most knowledgeable among the historians and scholars could not find references to such an event occurring at any point in Aroka's past, calling into question just how much damage the world had undergone during the First Firefall and the [[Age of Ice]]. At first, these [[Voidfall|Voidfalls]] were very brief events, but each year they grew longer and longer. Eventually, they lengthening into a full day in which temperatures dropped by the hour and creatures of the Depths emerged from their caverns and pits to raid the lightless surface. Within just a few years, nearly all cultures had adopted the [[Voidfall|Voidfalls]] as the end of the year, starting a new one when light once again returned to the world.
