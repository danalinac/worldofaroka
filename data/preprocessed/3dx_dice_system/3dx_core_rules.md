# 3dX Core Rules


---



3dX has undergone several revisions as it evolved from a d20 system to a three dice pool with the core mechanics shifting at each step of the way. After participating in [CRIT 2026](https://tabletop.events/conventions/crit-2026) and playing in ten different online games, each with their own TTRPG system, I took 3dX back to the drawing board and reworked the mechanics into something I believe is more engaging, more intuitive, and smoother to play. While the concept of the core rules are in place, I am still working through the wording for the rule set and dealing with edge cases and exploits in the rules. Once those are complete, they will be posted here and playtesting will begin for 3dX Dice System Version 0.2.
