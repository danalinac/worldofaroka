# Skyfarer

---



When the world below is too dangerous to travel on, some take on the risk of traveling through the skies instead of. Inspired by the advanced airborne vessels of the Age of Divinity, some genius and/or insane individuals have created craft capable of traversing through the air itself, safely bypassing any hazards on the lands or waters below. These Skyfarer are often haphazardly constructed, prone to mishap, and difficult to control, but in some Regions, such as the Sandsea, it's by far the safest mode of travel for those that can afford it.
