# <a href="obsidian://open?vault=Obsidian&file=Nature%2FPhenomena%2FVoidfall">Voidfall</a>

---



Traversing the skies in pairs and occasionally passing through the Voidgate to either rest or ward off the monsters of [[The Void|the Void]] depending on which culture's cosmology you subscribe to, the Three Suns keep watch over the [[World of Aroka|world of Aroka]], bathing it in light and warmth. Appearing as nothing more than dots of light in an otherwise empty sky, even the least observant of Arokans can understand their importance, especially when they pass through the Voidgate a cease to contribute their light and heat for a time.

Valkena is the brightest and by far the quickest of the three, passing through the Voidgate three time a year outside of the [[Voidfall]]. Days where it dominates the sky paint the sky in blue, sometimes violet, and its rise and set are marked by its gold, orange, and red hues.

Syrga is often difficult to distinguish from Valkena by those unaware of their respective movements. However, those that have taken the effort and time to track the two have identified that it passes through the Voidgate only once outside of the [[Voidfall]], making it far slower than its counterpart. Those foolish enough to compare the brightness of the two will also recognize that Syrga is ever so slightly dimmer than Valkena.

Xaraka is the easiest to discern as it is often, but not always, the smallest of the three. Even when it does appear to be larger than the other two suns, it is the least bright of the three and tends to wash pale the blues and violets of the other two, casting icy blue or white hues at dawn and dusk. The exception to this pattern is when Xaraka is the only star in the sky, painting it a deep, almost oceanic, blue. It is also the slowest of the three, passing through the Voidgate only during the [[Voidfall]].
