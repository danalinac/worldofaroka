#  About Aroka


---



I began writing stories for as long as I can remember. It started out as random short stories in fantasy, sci-fi, and modern superhero settings and slowly grew into a set of tales set in  a shared universe. I narrowed down the storytelling to fantasy and sci-fi with plenty of overlap and began to create a mythology that stretched from the lives of individual people to the events of the cosmos. I even tried writing a few "books" in that setting that were excellent for expanding upon the universe, but were severely lacking in quality.

Throughout highschool, college, and early adulthood, the majority of my time was spent pursuing my career and education, leaving little time for worldbuilding and writing, though I would still return to it on occasion and continued to develop its details in my head.

Around 2023, I switched careers and found myself able to dedicate more time to my interests and hobbies, so I began to dive back into the universe I had created, initially beginning work on a new world that eventually merged with the one I had created in my youth. I began running a highly modified d20 TTRPG set in this world for a small group of friends and family, using it to refine the mechanics of what would eventually become the 3dX Dice System and the [[World of Aroka]].

Since then, I have run three other campaigns set on the [[World of Aroka]] and even managed to bring one of them to completion with the others ended prematurely due to scheduling conflicts and other common enemies of TTRPGs. I continue to develop the universe, splitting my time between generating new content, reworking older content, and refining the 3dX Dice System. My goal is to complete work on a few new pages every week and publish them on Sunday, slowly building up the [[World of Aroka]] site and its many mysteries and wonders.

If there is anything in particular you want to know more about, please let me know at 3dxdice@gmail.com.
