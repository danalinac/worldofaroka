## Summary

The party awoke to tremors shaking the cavern. The Guide was missing, having departed during the night. Hours later, he returned—and revealed his true nature: a Pallian robotic host, one of many who had transferred their spirits into machines to survive the post‑War of Ashes world.

He warned that the Var’Yn were attempting to unthaw a colossal ancient warrior beneath a frozen lake near Pallium. In response, the Pallians were gathering energy to awaken their own unfinished titan. A new war loomed.

The Guide begged the party to help the Pallians by retrieving the power core of a captured Pallian war machine held by a Crab‑Kin colony.

The party agreed—for now.

They infiltrated the Crab‑Kin temple, silently eliminating guards until a massive robotic crab activated. The battle was vicious, nearly killing Ahgythah, but the party triumphed and extracted the power core.

Then a shadow dropped from the rafters: a Var’Yn scout.

He claimed the Var’Yn sought to protect the world from ancient forces that once defeated them, and needed Pallium’s technology to do so. He asked the party to betray the Guide and deliver the core to the Var’Yn instead.

The party weighed their choices:

- **Side with the Pallians** and help convert the world into machines.  
- **Side with the Var’Yn** and empower a conquering army.  
- **Or choose a third path.**

They chose the Var’Yn—at least for now—and agreed to delay or incapacitate the Guide to maintain the element of surprise.

The final fork in the road had been taken.
