###### AOR 542-5-70

At dawn, the Last Hope launched their assault on the Pillar while the Hermit shielded them from the flying soldiers of the Mosquito-Folk. The party slipped in through a side entrance and navigated the maze‑like interior, sneaking through abandoned chambers and dodging ancient traps.

On the second floor, they ambushed a Mosquito-Folk patrol before ascending to the third, where they discovered a horrific jail filled with mutilated creatures—failed experiments of the hive’s dark mage. In one cage, they found a wounded Ahedyn girl. Initially frightened of the party, the girl refused rescue or treatment, but Ahgythah was eventually able to calm her enough to tend to her wounds. However, before they could help her escape, the door to the jail slammed shut and all the cages opened. The party quickly crammed into the girl's cage and held the door shut while the monsters battled each other. When only one remained, the party quickly struck it down.

It took some effort, but the party was able to break open the door to the jail and quickly dispatched the Mosquito-Folk guards waiting on the other side with only one escaping. However, this proved to be to the party's benefit as the fleeing Mosquito-Folk led them straight to a staircase at the foot of a set of large stone doors that likely led into a throne room where the hive's rulers would be.

Not wishing to enter without reinforcements, the party waited for the Last Hope to fight their way up through the pillar, which didn't take long as more of the Mosquito-Folk had already fled before the ferocity of the Last Hope and the power of the Hermit.
