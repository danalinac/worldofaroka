##### AOR 542-5-13

Ahygthah spent the day recovering while per party continued to toil for the Church. Healers from the Church came and provided her what services they could and informed her that her patient was still recovering, but looked like he would make a full recovery.

Around mid-day, the Representative of the Church approached the party and asked if they would be interested in serving the Church in a different capacity. They didn't provide many details, but did specify that their combat proficiencies would be of great use in this new role and the Church would assist them in achieving their goal of reaching Pallium. They would have until the following morning to make their decision.

The party discussed their options. With multiple bounties likely having been put out for Tyche and the large sum of money they would require to charter vessel to Pallium, it looked like the Church's offer was the best path forward. They decided to sleep on it and rediscuss in the morning.

##### AOR 542-5-14

In the morning, the party reconvened and decided to take the Church up on their offer. They informed the Representative, whom told them to gather their things and following him. They did so and were led to the cavern Ahgythah had been taken to a couple nights before. There, the Representative told them that they needed to venture into the Sea of Darkness to either determine why the local resources used to charge the power cells of The Light were diminishing and find a way to stop it or find another source of fuel. This mission had been of the utmost priority for the Church, but their secret special operations unit had taken a few enough casualties to limit their effectiveness, which is why the Church was now seeking to hire the party to fill that role.

Though the party had many questions, very few were answered as the Church still wanted to keep most of the secrets to themselves, but let them know that they would be privy to more of their secrets if and when they returned from their mission. With no more answer to provide, the Representative told the party that they should rest for the remainder of the day as their ship would not arrive until The Light shut off.

Shortly after darkness fell over the Sea of Light, a small, quiet vessel entered the cavern. A fully-cloaked figure stood at its helm and quickly motIoned for the party to climb aboard. The figure introduced themselves only as the Guide and informed the party that they would travel without lights and that they should make no noise unless to alert the Others of danger. With that, they silently left the cavern and moved across the black waters. The party was not even able to see their hands in front of their own faces, so they relied on sound to detect any anomalies. They were unsure how the Guide was able to navigate without light, but didn't ask in order to maintain the silence.

After an hour, the party arrived at a small dock beneath a tower barely illuminated by a few torches and candles. Most of the party recognized this as the Tower of Light atop which the artificial sun sat. On the docks, Augustus, Helianth, and Jack were waiting for them as the Church had retrieved them from Tyche's home and brought them here to rejoin the party. Once everyone was aboard, they set out again. Despite not being able to see, the party could hear that they were traveling down a channel. 

After a half hour, they reach a gate that towered above them with torches faintly visible atop it. The gate creaked open, revealing a ruined city that had been repurposed as a maritime stronghold. Once inside, the Guide informed them that this was the Edge of Light, a stronghold manned by The Light Guard. The  all-volunteer order of highly trained maritime warriors share a single mission: prevent the monsters and peoples of the Sea of Darkness from entering the Sea of Light. Once docked, the Guide told them they would depart in an hour and that they should take this opportunity to purchase any supplies they believed they would need as there would be no on to trade with in the Sea of Darkness. As instructed, the party purchased additional equipment and rations, hoping it would be enough to last them until they returned.

Once there were done replenishing their supplies, the party returned to the boat and the Guide steered it through the waterways of the Edge of Light and out a set of doors equally imposing to the ones they had entered in by. They were also accompanied by a vessel of Light Guard, which utilized a single light at the front of their boat to see ahead. 

After a half hour, the party could hear that they were at the end of the channel and that a vast, dark ocean lay before them. The Light Guard vessel followed the short to the right and went out of sight. The Guide explained that The Light Guard had a lighthouse out here called the Edge Tower that they would light whenever someone wished to enter the Sea of Darkness. Most of the creatures of the region would be drawn to this light, allowing the vessel to cover as much distance as possible before catching their attention. The best they could hope for was that they could reach shallower waters where only the smaller creatures could follow, leaving the much larger and dangerous ones in the depths. The Guide also asked if the party wished to try and reach the shallows on the Other side of the depths that separated the Sea of Light from the Sea of Darkness or if they wanted to instead head for the Whispering Caverns. Both ways would be treacherous, but in different ways. Not sure of how well they could fend of aquatic creatures while inside a boat, the party elected to head for the Caverns as it would more significantly limit which creatures could pursue them.
