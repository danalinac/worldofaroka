##### AOR 542-4-70

Ready for whatever lay ahead, the hermit, the Last Hope, and the party ascended the stairs and began to push open the large metal doors. They were heavy and there was clearly debris stacked against it, but they slowly began to push it ajar. Once a large enough gap had been created, Ahgythah squeezed through to clear away the rubble and pull one of the doors open. She was instantly bombarded with a shower of arrows and stones, but her armor shielded her from harm long enough for her to pull open the door and let her allies in.

The throne room was a vast, dark space with blood-stained floors and grotesque piles of deteriorating flesh pressed up against its walls and pillars. A small army of suckers stood between them and what appeared to be the rulers of the hive. Several large and monstrous Suckers stood at the far end of the hall, some of which had been augmented with additional appendages, limbs, and other abominable features. Beside them stood a tall, lizard like being with two sets of arms and multiple heads magically grafted to its body. Even those not attuned to Magic could sense the power emanating from this being.

The battle was fierce. These Suckers proved more formidable than the ones the party previous faced and they were supported by mighty Husks the fought with abandon. When the first line of Suckers had been defeated, the dark mage began to animate the blood and rotting flesh scattered throughout the throne room, bringing forth masses of gore and viscera to attack and terrify the Last Hope.

During the battle, several members of the Last Hope proved themselves to be quite formidable, fighting alongside the party as they tore through waves of enemies. Je'Olt, an Axolotl-Kin, charged ahead with Perry, defending him against the shorter enemies while he took down the larger ones. Jorge, a Hyena-Kin, stood his ground against many enemies far larger than him, even falling several times before Ahgyhtha applied treated his wounds with Magic, causing him to regain his footing and continue to fight. Helianthonese (Hellianth), a Cat-Horse-Bryd, and Augustus, an Otter-Kin, fought their way along the wall to try and sneak past the main defense and strike the dark mage and the hive rulers. While unsuccessful in reaching their targets, they did manage to fend of some particular aggressive Husks without taking any injuries themselves. Additionally, the Ahedyn, who seemed to be fueled by a hatred for the Suckers, unleashed powerful blood conduit Magic on all that dared attack her, cutting down even the largest Husks with ease.

As the Suckers' numbers diminished, the dark mage attempted to escape, but was caught and quickly subdued by the party and their allies. His offensive Magic made him a dangerous adversary and even his defensive Magic made him nearly impervious to ranged attacks, but once he was within melee range, he succumbed quickly to the weapons of the Last Hope and the party. The rulers of the hive put up a good final fight, but they too fell. Thanks to the efforts of Ahgythah and the other healers, only six warriors of the Last Hope perished, though many were so injured that they would likely be unable to fight ever again.

Disgusted by the abominations that had been created within the pillar, the Last Hope began to formulate a plan to craft another explosive to bring down the pillar and bury all the evil created within beneath its rubble. The Hermit, not believing that would be enough, asked for time to search the pillar and cleanse it of evil before they brought it down. The Last Hope agreed and made preparations to return to Sanctum to treat their wounds and assess how many battle-ready warriors they still possessed. The party agreed to return to Sanctum as well to recover before continuing on their journey.
