Ione was a sharp‑eyed Fox‑Kin whose poise and precision made her presence felt long before she spoke. Born among the Dust‑Walkers, she learned to blend with both people and sand, to loosen a coin purse or a lock with equal ease, and to never trust anyone — especially those she cared about. She mastered the first two lessons effortlessly. Her failure to grasp the third left her shattered when a job went wrong and her crew abandoned her to take the fall. She escaped capture by wit and luck alone and swore that her former allies would one day regret not stabbing her in the back before they fled.

Before she could make good on that promise, she needed sharper skills and sturdier allies — people who could do the heavy lifting while she moved unseen between their shadows. In the dunes of the Dustveil, her white fur against white sand made her the perfect scout, and her unassuming charm made her the perfect thief. But in the forests of the Wild West, she had to push her limits to make others sure others saw what she wanted them to see. Lighthearted as she often seemed, there was a gleeful edge to her vengeance, a macabre delight in imagining what she might do when she finally caught up to those who wronged her. If she ever hoped to get close enough to drive a knife through their hearts, she needed to become someone who could vanish in plain sight, leaving only the faintest suggestion that she had ever been there at all.
