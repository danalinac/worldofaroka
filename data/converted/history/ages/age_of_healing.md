# Age of Healing

---



Following the devastation of the <a href="/pages/history/ages/age_of_war/" class="internal-link">Age of War</a>, the peoples of Aroka elected to set aside their differences and strive to build a better world. While conflict was still common, significant effort was put into settling disputes peaceful and a new world build on cooperation and shared prosperity was born. The technologies that had been used to lay waste to their enemies were converted into tolls of agriculture and industry, allowing the limited resources of Aroka to support larger populations and to protect this new world from anything that threatened it weather it be beast, the forces of nature, or those still seeking their own prosperity at the cost of others.

Whether due to the feats they achieved as a united people or mere fate, the people of Aroka were approached by beings that introduced themselves as gods from beyond the Void. They offered to grant knowledge and power in exchange for being allowed to walk Aroka's surface and study it. This new alliance would eventually blossom into the <a href="/pages/history/ages/age_of_divinity/" class="internal-link">Age of Divinity</a>.
