##### AOR 542-4-49

After party was awoken in the early morning by the sounds of howls and screams. Quickly peeking out of the ruins and down onto the camp the other Parties had set up and saw that it was overrun by a pack of Rippers that tore at both the living and the dead. While not familiar with this particular breed, Jack he encountered enough Rippers in the wilderness to know that these beasts were good at two things: swarming their prey and ripping it to shreds. He urged the party to pack up quickly and retreat deeper into the ruins, hoping that the creatures would be too distracted by their current prey to give chase. This prove futile, however, as the party hear the snarling and howling of the Rippers echoing through the corridors behind them.

The party realized they were being outpaced by the Rippers and decided to hold their ground in an area where the walls and ceiling of the ruins fell away to an immense chasm in the earth. A bridge led to the other side, but there were many places where it had deteriorated with time, making crossing, especially under duress, precarious. They took many wounds as the Rippers nipped at their flesh, tearing through their armor and shredding their clothing. However, the party was triumphant, killing just enough to send the rest running. Weary and unsure of when it would be safe to rest again, the party pushed onwards into the ruins. Serqet, while haven taken just as many wounds as the others, felt more ill than anticipated but pushed on regardless, unaware of the sickness she had contracted the pervious day.

Despite many opportunities to turn down countless corridors, they kept going straight, deeper into the mountains. Eventually, they saw light ahead and felt a frigid wind blowing past them. Eager to be out from underground, the hurried ahead. Once their eyes adjusted to the blinding light of day, the party found themselves standing atop an ancient structure looking out over a vast mountain range blanketed in snow. Crumbling structures dotted the landscape along with countless places the earth itself seemed to have collapsed into a void beneath the mountains.

Cautiously, the party descended a flight of stone stairs to what used to be a Road that once crossed with region. They could see through the holes in the earth that these was an immense cavern beneath this region that look to be filled with similar ruins as if a city had once existed both above and beneath the mountains here. If it weren't for the architecture, which indicated the ruins to be from the Age of Ice, they would have believed they had come upon the Lost City of Pallium, but these ruins were no where near advanced or old enough to belong to the legend. 

Overhead, they spotted large winged creatures circling and decided to find a way underground. They quickly found a place where the mixture of earth and rubble formed steep but navigable ramp into the cavern below, so they descended. Once beneath they earth, they realized that a labyrinth of crumbling earth and stone lay before them. This region, often called the Crumbling Labyrinth, seemed to be the remains of a once great city likely build during the Age of Ice and now overrun with Deep-Folk, Sect, and other subterranean monsters. They could hear distance crumbling and Roaring, but at least they didn't need to worry About being swooped down on by winged predators, hopefully.

Navigating the Labyrinth proved tricky, but the party used a tall tower with multicolored lights place around its middle terrace in the distance as a guide, slowing winding their way closer to it. As night fell, they came across the ruins of a small, subterranean village and took refuge in one of the more intact structures. 

During the night, they were ambushed by a Mouse-Kin, and Rat-Kin, and two Sect. Their assailants quickly realized that they underestimated the party and were dispatched. Their equipment suggested that they had preyed on previous explorers, killing them in their sleep and steeling their gear. The party was able to salvage some of their equipment and stood extra watch ensure they weren't surprised again.
