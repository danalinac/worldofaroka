##### AOR 542-5-16

After treating their wounds and resting, the party asked how they could earn the Quill's trust. They were informed that a nest of pincers deeper in the caverns was growing a bit too large. The Sects' presence helped deter unwanted creatures and peoples from wandering through the Whispering Caverns, but if their numbers began too great, they would start to attack members of the Quill. If the party could prune their numbers, the Quill would be willing to share some more information with them.

It didn't the party long to reach the pincers' cave. However, they were not prepared for how aggressive they were and were pushed back. They spent the rest of the day tending to their wounds and developing a new plan of attack.
