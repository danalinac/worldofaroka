###### AOR 542-4-39

Arriving at Kendathyl Warf, the party found the port overflowing with adventurers preparing for the race to Pallium. With lodging scarce, they camped outside the walls and soon discovered that the surrounding forest held both opportunity and danger.

###### AOR 542-4-40

Hunting Leapers earned them coin and rations, but also led to their arrest when stolen heirlooms were found among their spoils. After clearing their names, they attempted to catch the real thief—an Otter‑Kin who had been pilfering valuables from locals and travelers. Their success restored their honor, but a new mystery emerged: a skeletal guardian known as the Warden began stalking their camp at night.

###### AOR 542-4-41

Seeking answers, the party visited the Warf’s library, where they met O’Ard, an Overseer of the Knights of the Scroll. He revealed fragments of lore about the Warden—an ancient being predating the First Firefall—and granted Jack a token of the Knights.

That night, the party attempted to confront the Warden using Serqet’s improvised light‑amplifying device. They briefly illuminated the creature, revealing a massive quadrupedal construct of stone and metal with bladed limbs and a mask‑like face. The encounter ended with Perry wounded and the Warden retreating into the darkness.

###### AOR 542-4-42

Despite their curiosity in the Warden, the party decided they could waste no more time. They gathered supplies, purchased a pack lizard, and prepared to leave the Warf behind—ready to begin their ascent into the Lost Mountains.
