###### AOR 542-4-53

After four days of recovery, the party set out with Ton and a contingency of warriors to gather ingredients for an explosive powerful enough to clear the Beetle‑Folk tunnel. Their path led them across a crumbling bridge fort occupied by Scorpion-Folk. A stealth attack turned into a diplomacy attempt on the part of Serqet, but that devolved into a bloody skirmish, costing the life of one warrior and forcing the group to rest before continuing.

###### AOR 542-4-54 to 55

The party spent the day resting and waiting for reinforcements from Sanctum to man the fort.

###### AOR 542-4-56

The Fungal Forest was a glowing, fog‑shrouded expanse of towering mushrooms and bioluminescent spores. The party quickly gathered most of the needed materials, but the final ingredient—a rare purple crystalline fungus—was guarded by strange plant‑like creatures that hunted by sound.

A misstep triggered a swarm attack. The party fled through the fog as hallucinations and confusion set in. Perry fell into the toxic mist but managed to escape before succumbing. A massive root‑and‑branch monstrosity emerged behind them, forcing a desperate sprint back toward Sanctum. Two more warriors were lost in the escape. 

###### AOR 542-4-57

Exhausted and shaken, the survivors stayed the night at the fort before returning to Sanctum with the necessary ingredients. Ton immediately began crafting the explosive, determined to honor the sacrifices made in the forest.

###### AOR 542-4-58-60

The party tended to their wounds and did their best to assure the Last Hope that the loss of life over the past few missions were unfortunate, but necessary for a better future. While the leaders of the Last Hope were not convinced, enough of its warriors believe in the party to join them and began making preparations for a return to the Beetle-Folk fortress with Ton's new explosive, assuming she didn't blow herself up in her lab before then.
