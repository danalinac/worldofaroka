## Summary

Ahgythah recovered from her ordeal while the rest of the party continued to toil. The Church offered them a new mission: venture into the Sea of Darkness to discover why the resources used to power the Light were dwindling.

The party accepted.

At nightfall, a silent vessel arrived, piloted by a cloaked figure known only as the Guide. They traveled without light, navigating by sound alone. After an hour, they reached the Tower of Light, where Jack, Augustus, and Helianth rejoined them.

The Guide led them through a fortified maritime city—the Edge of Light—manned by the Light Guard, elite warriors dedicated to preventing anything from the Sea of Darkness from entering the Sea of Light.

After resupplying, the party departed through massive gates into the black waters. The Guide explained their options:  
– attempt to cross the deep ocean and reach the far shallows, or  
– enter the Whispering Caverns, a labyrinthine waterway that limited which monsters could pursue them.

The party chose the caverns.

Ahead lay darkness, whispers, and the unknown.
