##### AOR 542-5-20

As promised, the party set a trap for the Guide, hurling rocks and chunks of rubble at his boat until it sank. As an advanced robot, the Guide was not drowned, but only delayed as it began to climb up the submerged portion of the structure, albeit slowly. Not long after, the Var'Yn scout returned with a larger Var'Yn that could fly the party members to a tunnel entrance higher up the cavern wall.

After walking through the tunnel for several hours and somehow managing not to irritate the Var'Yn enough to start a fight, they emerged into the evening light of day. Before them lay a vast forested valley surrounded by snowy mountains. To the east, a partially collapsed volcano trickled streams of lava, scorching the surrounding land and filling the sky with ash and smoke. To the west, they saw a mountain dotted with metallic spired rising into the sky; the Lost City of Pallium.

The party continued to follow the Var'Yn, reaching the volcano just before the last of the suns' light disappeared over the horizon. The glow of lava provided sufficient light to see as the party ventured inside, Roasting in the heat. Fortunetly, they didn't need to go very far as they were left to wait for the leader of the Var'Yn to see them. 

Before long, a golden Var'Yn riding atop a significantly larger crimson Var'Yn landed before them and dismounted. Kal'Rah, leader of this expedition, informed the party that time was short and that they needed to act quickly. They informed the party that the Var'Yn were descendants of a void-faring ancestry that strived to preserve all forms of life. They had come to Aroka to do the same, but were defeated by several mysterious forces with comparable technologies, resulting in the War of Ashes. They believe these forces are still present on the world and that they are also trying to rebuild their armies and rise up again. To counter this, the Var'Yn need the technologies hidden within Pallium and they want the party to help them claim it.

Not completely sure which of the two factions to believe and which would probably cause more harm to the world when the Other was removed, the party decided that not being turned into robots was a good enough reason to side with the Varyn. They spent the night recovering form their wounds and resting, a process that was expedited by Var'Yn healers. The power cell they had collected from the robotic crab was rigged with a time explosive, setting into motion a plan to deliver it to Pallium's secret weapon and detonating it. 

##### AOR 542-5-21

At first light, the party mounted winged Var'Yn and were flown over the valley, circling around to the southern side of Pallium. As they flew, the volcano erupted out towards Pallium, scorching the forests as forging a path of fire and lava. The winged Var'Yn explained that this would allow their foot soldiers to absorb energy from the burning landscape and fight harder. This level of Destruction for a tactical advantage gave the party pause, but it was too late to change paths.

While in flight, Jack also became aware of a foreboding presence entering the region. He had noticed it before, but never this strong. The god trapped in his decanter urged him onward. A new threat was approaching and he needed to find a new host, preferably one of Pallium's hosts, to hope to stand against it.

Landing on one of the spires of Pallium, the party entered the ancient city. The majority of its defenses had been reallocated to fighting the Var'Yn, so they encountered no resistance until they reached the heart of the city where their secret weapon was being prepared. A massive robotic beast with countless hoses and wires feeding into it sat, ready to awaken and go to war. Numerous Pallians hurriedly worked to make the final preparations. Once spotted, the alarm was raised.

The fight was challenging as the Pallians realized that their secret weapon was at risk. However, it quickly became obvious that something else was approaching. Jack could feel the Slayer approaching, so he decided to act, releasing the god in his decanter into the robotic beast. It Roared to life, powered by the souls Jack had collected from those he had slain. Perry, witnessing the betrayal, attacked Jack and pummeled him close to death, but did not land the killing blow. The unchain god chomped down on Jack, consuming his flesh and spirit in a final act a service.

In the chaos that followed, the Pallians close enough to respond to the intrusion were slain and the new god had established that it could not be defeated by mere mortals. Even the Slayer, an ancient husk of armored magic and technologies that existed to purge the world of monsters, could do little against it, though it continued to trudge after the robotic beast no matter how many times it was knocked down. In the end, the party was persuaded by the newly freed god to let it go as it wanted neither the Pallians or the Var'Yn to claim the city's technologies for themselves. This third option seemed the least of Destructive of the three, so the party elected to part ways.

What happened after the party left Pallium is unknown, but it is believed that they found a village within the unburnt portions of the forested valley and lived among a community of <a href="/pages/nature/fauna/sapients/dryn/" class="internal-link">Dryn</a>, settling down and living a life of relative peace. It was not clear whether the Pallians, the Var'Yn, or the new god won, though there are still stories of robotic beasts, deranged Var'Yn, and an unrelenting hunter stalking the valley.
