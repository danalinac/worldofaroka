## Summary

The party returned to the valley with news of the fallen Seekers. The elders rewarded them with supplies and granted them continued refuge. But peace lasted only a day.

A company of armed strangers camped by the lake, requesting an audience with Jack. Their armor bore the crossed‑swords‑and‑scroll insignia of the Knights of the Scroll—Inquisitors.

One approached alone and demanded Jack surrender the Decanter, claiming it was too dangerous to remain in mortal hands. Jack refused. The Inquisitor attacked with magic, but Jack struck first, binding him with spectral tentacles. The party rushed to help, and the Inquisitor was slain before reinforcements arrived.

A fierce battle followed. The mercenaries and remaining Inquisitors were highly trained, and the party suffered severe injuries. Only two enemies survived—one fled, and one Tortoise‑Kin was captured.

Interrogation revealed grim news: the Knights would send a Slayer, a legendary executioner, to retrieve the Decanter and eliminate the party. Jack, enraged, used the Decanter’s power to rip out the captive’s soul—horrifying his companions.

The elders, alarmed by the violence and the use of magic near their gates, banished the party and all Sanctum refugees from the valley. With no choice, the group gathered their belongings and prepared to descend into the caverns once more.

The Slayer was coming.
