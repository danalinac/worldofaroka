# The Void

---



All that sits above Aroka is a vast, empty Void with only a handful of objects that punctuate its absolute darkness. The most prominent of these are the <a href="/pages/nature/phenomena/three_suns/" class="internal-link">Three suns</a>: Valkena, Syrga, and Xaraka. These small glowing orbs provide light and warmth to the world during the day, moving at varying rates across the sky. It is not uncommon for them to pass each other, sometimes sitting so close together that they appear to be a single object. Their light is persistent apart for when they near the center of the sky and flicker out, appearing several days later at another point along their path. 

Academics and Scholars have tried their best to understand what causes the suns to vanish and reemerge undaunted. It seems that they all disappear and reappear in the same general areas and always when the suns appear the dimmest and smallest, leading many to speculate that they may grow so faint that they are no longer visible to the naked eye during this period. Others suspect that they become obscured by another, unseen object or group of objects, while those that error more towards the mythical believe that they pass through the Veil and into the unseen world for purposes ranging from resting from fending of the creatures of the Void or going to do battle with them directly.

The only other object visible in the sky is a faint fourth red sun only visible at night and not even every night. Those that have studied this object, which is most commonly called Mera,  observe that it will will appear every night for a year or so before vanishing for many more. When close enough to Aroka, it causes Red Nights. Though the world isn't nearly as bright during the day, the red glow is sufficient to dispel the usual suffocating blackness. Strange events regularly occur during these nights, though the validity of these claims is often called into question due to how outlandish they are.

Astute observers will also notice, when the first or last suns of the day near the horizon, a silvery band arching from horizon to horizon. Whether this is the trick of the light or another object sitting within the void is debated, but the Voidwall is observed frequently enough to not be a figment of imagination. Many believe it to be a barrier that keeps the Void from descending upon and consuming Aroka.  Some scholars point to records of a fifth, far larger and paler sun that moved entirely separate from the motion of the others and, on occasion, was visible even at night. Their theories suggest that the Voidwallis a trail left behind by fifth sun, proposing that the other suns leave similar trails that they would be too small to see due to their significantly smaller size.  All such records predate the First Firefall, so many assume that it was perhaps one of the gods that fled at the start of the apocalypse.
