# Weaver

---



Weaver are spiderlike creatures typically ranging in sprawl from a half meter in diameter to 20 meters in diameter. While some subspecies are known to hunt, the majority of them weave webs in high traffic Areas to catch their prey. The Various species also have a venomous bite with effects ranging from induce lethargy to paralysis with the goal being to incapacitate their prey and then drain them of fluids. These creatures are most often encountered underground, but will sometimes surface if they are unable to catch enough prey to survive beneath the earth.
