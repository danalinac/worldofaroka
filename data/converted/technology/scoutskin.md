# Scoutskin

---



Scoutskin is a loose outer garment designed to help Ohedyn scouts blend in to the jungles of the <a href="/pages/nature/fauna/sapients/scourge/" class="internal-link">Scourge</a> Wilderness of Sona. Variants of the Scoutskin are designed for specific biomes, but the most common one is for jungle and dense forest environments.
