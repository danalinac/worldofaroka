# The Decanter

---



Little is known about the Decanter apart from sparse encounters during AOR 1083 and 1084 between the <a href="/pages/locations/regions/sandsea/" class="internal-link">Sandsea</a> and the Lost Mountains. Reports from this period say it was in the possession of a Harekin accompanied by a Scorpionkin, though there were often other individuals associated with the pairing, the most common of which was a Platypuskin. While their credibility is in question, a handful of reports from Morydal and Amyhra claim that the Harekin used to be a member of a delving crew that was last seen venturing onto the <a href="/pages/locations/regions/sandsea/" class="internal-link">Sandsea</a>. Some went so far as to include that the crew had not been heard or seen from since.

It was not uncommon for encounters with the Decanter to be closely followed by inquiries from local governments as well as mysterious newcomers asking an assortment of oddly specific questions. Whatever the nature of the object, it seems clear that it did not travel unnoticed and left a trail of unease. The last confirmed sighting of it is believed to have been at Kendathyl Wharf.
