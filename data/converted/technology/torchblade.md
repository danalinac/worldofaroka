# Torchblade

---



Whether fending off a fiend or braving places unknown to the Voidlights, a Torchblade is one of the rarer tools carried by adventurers. Despite its advanced design, it doesn't originate from Cogys, which has led many to believe it is a relic from the <a href="/pages/history/ages/age_of_divinity/" class="internal-link">Age of Divinity</a> .
 
At first glance, a Torchblade appears to be a small, decorative torch with a slot in its hilt. Mondern tinkerers have managed to bridge the technologies gap, using various batteries or power sources to power the Torchblade, though many speculate this solution is far from sufficient to use its full potential.
 
There are two known uses of the Torchblade: as a torch and as a blade, as its name suggests. While used as a torch, it casts bright light out to an adjustable distance in a hue aligned with its power source. When used as a blade, the energy consolidates into the equivalent of a longsword. It is believed that there are more functions than this, especially for the more elaborate variants, but those secrets have illuded even the greatest engineers of Cogys.
