Summary

After resting from their battle with the Rippers, the party asked the Secret Quill how they could further earn trust. Fen Fith explained that a nearby nest of Pincers had grown too large. While the Quill tolerated a small population—useful for deterring intruders—an oversized nest posed a threat to their scouts and couriers. The party was tasked with “pruning” the colony.

They reached the nest quickly, but the Pincers were far more aggressive than expected. The creatures swarmed in overwhelming numbers, forcing the party to retreat under a barrage of claws and snapping mandibles. Several members suffered deep wounds, and even Ahgythah struggled to keep everyone standing.

Back at the Echoing Isle, the group spent the remainder of the day treating injuries and devising a new strategy. Their first attempt had failed, but the mission remained: reduce the Pincer population before it spilled into Quill territory.

Tomorrow, they would try again—smarter, tighter, and ready for revenge.
