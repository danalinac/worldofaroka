Mantle of the Fallen World was a 25‑session campaign centered on a perilous expedition into the Lost Mountains and the long‑buried mysteries surrounding the Lost City of Pallium — a technological marvel of the Age of Divinity whose final days were marked by desperate attempts to halt the world’s destruction.

For centuries, the Lost Mountains remained too treacherous to traverse. Only in the 542nd year of the Age of Reclamation did a new path open, drawing adventurers, scholars, and powerful factions into the Ruined Sea region. Among them were three unlikely companions — Jack, Serqet, and Perry — whose simple expedition rapidly escalated into a chain of crises.

Hunted through collapsing ruins, entangled in the struggles of Sanctum, drawn into the politics of a hidden Dryn valley, and cast into the depths of the Sunless Sea, the party confronted forces far older than the Age of Reclamation. Their journey forced uneasy alliances, difficult sacrifices, and confrontations with ancient powers awakening beneath the world.

From the horrors buried beneath the Crumbling Labyrinth to the metallic guardians of Pallium itself, the group uncovered truths that challenged everything they believed about the past — and the future. As factions clashed and long‑dormant powers stirred, the party faced choices that would determine whether Pallium’s legacy became salvation, ruin, or something far more complicated.
