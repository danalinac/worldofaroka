When the first rays of sunlight graced the Crumbling City, the Last Hope launched their attack on the central pillar. The Hermit used its Magic to protect the army from the air, allowing them to focus on battling only on foot. With the siege underway, the party rushed to the blocked entrance they had selected the previous day, quickly cleared away enough rubble to gain entry, and slipped inside.

Cautiously, the party explored the maze-like interior of the pillar, encountering long-abandoned chambers and ancient traps. Eventually, they located a spiral staircase leading up to the second level. This new level was much more open, seeming to have been a gathering hall with a stagnant stone fountain at its center and the remains of long deteriorated wooden furniture. The staircase to the third level was easy to find, but the party detected Suckers descending it, so they hid and waited to ambush the patrol. The fight was over nearly as quickly as it had started and the party hurried up the stairs.

The third floor was more similar to the first in that it consisted of many passages that branched and rejoined with no detectable pattern. After wandering around for a few minutes, the party found what appeared to be a jail room lined with cages. Several creatures that looked like they had suffered wounds too significant to have survived paced within their cells, gargling and growling like crazed beasts. Several benches along one wall was covered in strange tools, all of which looked like they had been used to conduct unthinkable experiments on these creatures.

In one cage, the party found a small figure curled up in its back corner. It appeared to be a young female Dryn covered in fresh wounds and barely able to keep her head up. Cautiously, the party broke the lock on the cell and appRoached the Dryn. She seemed frightened, but lacked the strength to fight back. Ahgythah began treating her wounds and assuring her that they meant her no harm. Though it did not seem as if the Dryn spoke Common or any of the other Languages members of the party spoke, she did seem to understand that they were not there to hurt her, so she let Ahgythah heal her both with magical and mundane means. While treating her wounds, Ahgythah discovered that she was not a Dryn but an Ahedyn, indicating that she may be far older than she appeared and likely possess significant magical capabilities.

Before the party could investigate the rest of the jail room, the main door slammed shut and all the cages swung open. Realizing they had fallen into a trap, the party rushed into the Ahedyn’s cage and closed the door behind them. The monsters tore at each other ravenously until only one remained. Rushing out of their own cage and attacking together, the party quickly took down the last monster, though they were still trapped in the jail room.

With the Ahedyn’s wounds treated as well as they could given the amount of time they had, Ahgythah and Parry pounded on the head door to the jail room, eventually knocking it off of its hinges. A group a suckers were waiting for them on the other side, but clearly had not anticipated the fighting prowess of the party and were quickly dealt with. One managed to escape and disappeared into the maze. Tracking the drops of blood left behind by the fleeing sucker, the party was able to locate a wide stone staircase leading up to a set of large metal doors. Moments later, the Hermit and the warriors of the Last Hope that were still in good enough condition to fight arrived. Ahgythah and the other healers treated as many wounds as they could and prepared to the assault on the throne room.
