AOR 542-4-61

Fully recovered and ready for the next leg of their journey, the party made final preparations to return to the Beetle-Folk fortress with Ton’s new explosive. Serqet elected to remain behind as she was conflicted about needing to kill the Scorpion-Folk at the fort on their way to the Fungal Forest. Ahgythah, a Sheep-Kin born shortly before the First Firefall and Sanctum’s primary healer, chose to join the party in her place, eager to hone her healing skills and escape the Labyrinth. She had observed the party’s ability to take many wounds and still survive, which would offer her plenty of practice and likely a chance to learn new healing skills. With Ton’s explosive complete, the group set out with Arxst and fifteen warriors to clear the collapsed Beetle‑Kin tunnel.

The explosive shattered the blockage, but instead of Beetle‑Kin, the party found the tunnels eerily empty. Webbing soon revealed the truth: a massive colony of Weavers had overrun the area. Splitting briefly to scout the three branching passages, the party was ambushed almost immediately.

Dozens of small Weavers swarmed them, their venomous bites sedating warriors one by one. Larger Weavers struck from the shadows, forcing the group to retreat to the tunnel entrance and form a defensive bottleneck. Just as the tide seemed to turn, a colossal Weaver emerged—its body filling the entire passage.

The battle was brutal. Many warriors fell unconscious from venom, but Ahgythah’s centuries of healing experience saved all but three. With the colossal Weaver slain, the party pushed forward and found the far fortress abandoned. Reinforcements from Sanctum were summoned to secure the newly opened route deeper into the Labyrinth.

AOR 542-4-62 to 63

The party chose to remain in place and recover from their wounds, giving Rasat time to fly and ahead and scout a path forward. No one from the Last Hope had been to this part of the Crumbling Labyrinth, since before the quakes, so much of the terrain had changed and their maps were no longer accurate.
