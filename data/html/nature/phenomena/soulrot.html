Soulrot

Often called the Curse of the Careless, Soulrot is a magical affliction associated with excessive magic use as well as magical mishaps. Those that contract it begin to dry out and lose their sanity, replacing their flesh with a thick, bone-like material with soul energy coursing through it and animating it. The rate of this transformation varies on the individual and how much magic they practice, but greater use is always correlated with a quicker transformation, even if the magic use is in an attempt to slow or reverse the descent. When the host eventually loses their wit, they are drawn to souls and magical energies with an unwavering hunger, feeding on them to further the change. By that point, bystanders must choose to either destroy or banish the host in hopes that its infection will end there. The disease is rare, poorly documented, and incurable, leading to the discouragement of magical practices except by the most well-trained and experienced in many parts of the world.
