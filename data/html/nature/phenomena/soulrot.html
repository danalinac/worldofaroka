Soulrot

Soulrot is a magical affliction sometimes associated with excessive Magic use as well as magical mishaps. Those that contract it begin to dry out and lose their sanity, replacing their flesh with a thick, bone-like material with soul energy coursing through it and animating it. They are drawn to souls and magical energies and can absorbent them. The disease is very rare and not well documented, but it has contributed to the discouragement of magical practices except by the most well-trained and experienced.
