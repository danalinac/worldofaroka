Seasons

Regardless of the calendar used by the various civilizations of Aroka, most can agree on the general seasons, which serve as excellent time markers for international trade as their effects are felt regardless of where in the world one is. Instead of summarizing countless different methods of tracking the passage of time, below is a list of key events throughout the year with an approximate day of occurrence. This assumes that the first day of the year is the one on which sunlight first graces Aroka’s face after the annual Voidfall. With the duration of the Voidfalls increasing year over year, the events may actually occur up to a day earlier than expected based on the previous year’s calendar.

Voidfrost

Day 1: This marks the first day of the year as one or more of the Three Suns returns to cast their light and warmth upon Aroka. Despite light returning, temperatures continue to drop in the wake of the Voidfall.

Frostthaw

Day 30: This is often when the world is at its coldest following the Voidfall. For those that survived the days of darkness and following chill, this marks the turning point for their struggles and that they are likely to persist another year.

Day 45: Valkena and Syrga appear as one in the sky.

Day 52: Valkena and Xaraka appear as one in the sky.

First Bloom

Day 97: Syrga and Xaraka appear as one in the sky.

Day 126: Valkena flickers out, cooling and dimming Aroka slightly until it reemerges two days later.

First Blaze

Day 171: This is the longest day of the year, being around four hours longer than the shortest day of the year.

Day 210: Valkena and Syrga again appear as one in the sky.

Day 224: This is the first of the two hottest days of the year, marking the end of one of Aroka’s two annual warm periods and warning of the coming winter. Not long afterward, temperatures will begin to drop rapidly.

Frostfall

Day 251: Syrga flickers out, returning four days later.

Day 253: Valkena flickers out, emerging two days later with Syrga. The loss of light and warmth from both Valkena and Syrga result in the steepest drop in temperature outside of the Voidfall.

Day 255: With Xaraka centered in the sky and both Valkena and Syrga return to the sky, all light sources are in the same position in the sky, resulting in the shortest day of the year.

Deepfrost

Day 280: After nearly thirty days of consistently dropping temperatures, it finally reaches its lowest in the depths of winter, reaching temperatures even colder than following the Voidfall.

Day 297: Valkena and Syrga appear as one in the sky.

Second Bloom

Day 380: Valkena again flickers out, returning two days later. This results in a similar dimming and temperature drop to the one experienced on day 126.

Day 405: Syrga and Xaraka appear as one in the sky.

Second Blaze

Day 455: Valkena and Xaraka appear as one in the sky.

Day 464: Valkena and Syrga appear as one in the sky.

Day 475: This is the second of the hottest days of the year, carrying the same warning as the first that winter is coming, though this one is far more foreboding as it also warns that the Voidfall is nigh.

Voidhark

Day 502: Xaraka flickers out, warning that the Voidfall is approaching.

Day 505: Syrga flickers out, warning that the Voidfall is imminent.

Day 507: Valkena flickers out, beginning the Voidfall.
