The Decanter

The Decanter is a mysterious object recovered from a ruin in the Sandsea of Duva by Jack, a Harekin adventurer guided to and through the ruins by Serqet, a Scorpionkin native of the Sandsea. Its origin is unknown, but it seems to be magical in Nature. Those that have spoken to Jack say the he claims it to be the home of a fallen god seeking to return to power. The individual has questionable sanity, so most are uncertain of the claim, but the object seems magical enough to attract the attention of everyone from the common thief to secret Organizations.

History It was recovered from a ruin beneath the Sandsea of Duva by an adventurer and has been spotted in Morydal, Amyhra, Ohedyn, and Kendathyl. It is believe to have been taken into the Lost Mountains. Nothing is know of its History beyond this.

Significance If the claims of its possessor are true, it contains a ancient god; And, if the actions of its possessor are anything to go by, it is a malicious one.
