On AI Use

I understand that the use of AI is highly controversial due to how disruptive it has been to nearly every industry, its unclear use of intellectual property, and the threat it poses to art in particular. As a result, I want to be crystal clear about how and to what extent I have utilized the technology.

Firstly, I believe AI is a tool and, like any tool, it can be used to harm or help. We see countless examples of how it is causing harm in the media and news, but we don’t often see the benefits it can provide if used wisely. My goal is to use the tool only so far as it provides a benefit to this project and to the creative industry, and to hold back from applying it in a way that causes harm.

To do this, I do not use AI to generate new ideas or write any of my work. I have used it to help work through the coding challenges of setting up and populating the website as well creating tools that I use to help keep the details and elements of the World of Aroka in order. It has been an effective aide for catching conflicts in my storytelling or worldbuilding, pointing out grammatical or spelling errors beyond what most word processors will notice, helping me develop and remain consistent in my tone and writing style, and generating summaries of already-existing content. In the future, I plan on using it to help keep the history and rules of the 3dX Dice System and World of Aroka consistent, informing me when I contradict something I have previously established, or notify me when I introduce something that doesn’t fit with the world thus far. In none of these use-cases will it ever create anything new about the world; only provide feedback on conflicts and inconsistencies or summarize content I have already created.

When it comes to artwork, I think that human-made art is by far superior, but there is also a place where AI can be used to generate concepts or placeholders, especially for new creators that don’t have the resources, skills, or time to create the artwork on their own or commission someone else to do it. It would seem that I am currently in that position, but I still feel hesitant about using AI art on this site. I can be helpful for visualizing how things might look and playing around with various art styles, but I only feel comfortable with limiting that to my personal behind the scenes use. Rest assured; when artwork is added to this site, I will explicitly state who the creator of that art is and what tools were used in the creation of that art.

If there is ever any AI-generated artwork on this site, it will be abundantly clear that is was generated by AI, that it is a placeholder, and that it will be replaced by human-made art as soon as it is made available. I would prefer that all the artwork be human-made, but that requires resources, skills, and time I don’t currently have, though I hope this project grows to the point where it can generate revenue that can go into supporting human creators and using their incredible talents to visually represent the World of Aroka.
