Support the Project

Thus far, 3dX and the World of Aroka are labors of love. I have not implemented any monetization options or strategies and have brought this project to life at my own expense. If that is what the future of the project remains, I will still continue to work on it as I thoroughly enjoy the process and hope that others will experience even a fraction of the enjoyment I feel making it a reality.

However, if you want to help, any support, either in the form of constructive feedback, fan-art, or monetary support, I would greatly appreciate it. If you are interested in contributing in any of these ways, you can reach me at 3dxdice@gmail.com. If you choose to provide monetary support, please let me know what you would like those funds to go towards, whether it be adding additional features to the site, expanding on additional content, or commissioning artwork. My professional career pays well, so any funds provided to this site will go directly into project. If there is enough interest in this type of support, I will look into streamlining the process through a platform like Patreon.
