My Inspirations

There are countless franchises and stories that have influenced my work, but some have had a greater impact than others.

I think the first example of storytelling and worldbuilding that inspired me to start telling my own was Lego’s Bionicle. Legos dominated my childhood and still have a solid hold of my imagination and wallet as an adult, with Bionicle having a special place in my heart for the incredible designs and sets. Unfortunately, the story was secondary to the toys, so I didn’t fully grasp the breadth and depth of the story. This changed around 2023 when I found DuckBricks’ Bionicle Retold series that laid out the entire Bionicle timeline. I had no idea how intricate of a universe they had created and how well they had dropped clues and hints into the larger story through it’s decade-long release schedule. It is overflowing with creative and imaginative ideas that inspired my work on the World of Aroka.

One of the other more significant influences on my storytelling was the Inheritance Cycle by Christopher Paolini. I was inspired by the young author’s intricate and compelling world, barely able to put down the books once I picked them up. My goal at the time was to also publish my own works and join the ranks of teen authors. Obviously, that didn’t happened, but this effort did kickstart my love for storytelling and worldbuilding.

Another major influence of mine is the Endless Universe by Amplitude Studios. On the surface, these games seems like another collection fantasy/sci-fi 4X strategy games, but the storytelling and worldbuilding is far more complex and intriguing than is apparent. Digging into the histories and lore of the factions and their place in the setting is highly rewarding and filled to the brim with unique concepts that set it apart from other franchises in the same vein.

While these three franchises hold the majority of the influence and inspiration for the World of Aroka, there are still more that also had a hand in sparking my imagination or encouraging me to continue pursuing this endeavor of creativity.

Arcane by Riot Games, Borderlands by Gearbox, Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 by Sandfall Interactive, the Cosmere by Brandon Sanderson, Darkest Dungeon by Red Hook Studios, Divinity by Larian Studios, Doom by id Software, His Dark Materials by Philip Pullman, Horizon by Guerrilla Games, Lord of the Rings by J.R.R. Tolkien, RWBY by Monty Oum & Rooster Teeth, Star Wars by George Lucas
