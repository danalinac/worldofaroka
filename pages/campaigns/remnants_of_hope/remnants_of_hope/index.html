Remnants of Hope

The Remnants of Hope campaign is the predecessor to the Mantle of the Fallen World campaign and was the first campaign to use the earliest versions of the 3dX Dice System. It began with a mismatched group of adventurers consisting of the Horse-Narwhal-Bryd Elsie, Dryn Flipbart, the Snake-Kin Lado, the Otter-Kin Roxy, and the Tiger-Kin Tony, found themselves in the possession of the secretive Remnant after an unprecedented Gaunt attack during the First Light Festival at Sanctuary. Desperate times called for desperate measures and the Remnant began mobilizing all the people of the Fractyr Unity to find a way to stop this threat. The party was tasked with traveling north to the more defensible mountain city of Sol.

When they arrived, the party faced something that had previously been considered impossible: Gaunt-infected Anthropes and Dryn. Typically, those infected and not cured of Gaunt quickly deteriorated, but this new strain seemed to be able to keep their hosts alive and functional. After cutting down these new abominations, the party was tasked with retrieving a rare plant used in curing the Gaunt infection. This took them to Lifespring Village, a mountainside hot spring home to a peaceful community of outcasts and wanders that wanted nothing to do with the affairs of the Fractyr Unity.

To gain the favor of the village elders and be allowed to trade for the rare plant, the party needed to deal with a Shoulshark that had travelled far further up river than was normal. During this hunt, the party discovered a cult of Anthropes seemingly trying to infect the Shoulshark with Gaunt. The fight was challenging, but the party prevailed but learned little about the mysterious cult working to propagate the Gaunt. Though the party had earned the privilege of trading for the rare plant, they had drawn the attention of the cult, putting Lifespring in danger. Elsie, Lado, Roxy, and Tony elected to remain at the village to help defend it.

Upon returning to Sol and being granted entry, the Gator-Kin Melinoe and Dryn Sarah joined the party. At the order of Ozura, the leader of the Remnant, they ventured into the mines beneath the mountain city, following a map to travel beyond the limits of the mines and into uncharted parts of the Undermount. Their mission was to find the Lost City of Pallium and reclaim its advanced weaponry to help in the war against the Gaunt. Here, they discovered crumbling ruins of ancient cities an colonies of fearsome creatures hunting in the dark. Eventually, they emerged from the depths and found themselves in the ruins of a much more recent civilization overrun with all manor of Sect. Here, they can in contact with Xyde, the leader of the Refuge Outcasts This company of elite Dryn warriors kept the Rising Hive confined to the Infested City so that what fertility remained in the Lost Valley could survive.

At this point, I bought a home that required some renovations, so this game was put on pause. When I was finally able to return, the players wanted to continue the story, but from a different approach. This birthed the Mantle of the Fallen World campaign, which approached the same overall goal of reaching the Lost City of Pallium but as independent adventurers starting at the northern end of the Lost Mountains instead of the southern end.

To make the two campaigns fit together, the Remnants of Hope party found themselves unable to safely navigate the Lost Valley due to the dangerous machines defending Pallium, perilous wildlife retreating into the ever-shrinking habitable areas of the valley, and treacherous Var’Yn that were triggering volcanic eruptions to raze the landscape of obstacles and fuel their siege of Pallium. The party would choose to settle in the valley and do all they could to make it a better home for themselves and the Dryn of Refuge. The party of the Mantle of the Fallen World campaign would ultimately make the same decision after the Battle for Pallium.
