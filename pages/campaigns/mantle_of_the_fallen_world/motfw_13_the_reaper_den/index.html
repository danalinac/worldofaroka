MOTFW 13. THE REAPER DEN

Summary

With the suns setting and Reapers emerging from the pit, the party desperately searched for shelter. A hooded figure revealed herself as a Sucker outcast and offered refuge in exchange for blood. With no other options, the party agreed, allowing her to feed on several members in return for safety, potions, and knowledge of the cellar’s magical lock.

Throughout the night, Reapers prowled the ruins, their shrieks echoing through the Crumbling City. The cellar door held, but the threat was unmistakable: the Reapers were awake.

At dawn, the party returned to Sanctum and warned the Last Hope. Panic spread—if the Reapers reached Sanctum, the settlement would fall. The party rallied warriors from both Sanctum and Lex‑Gate Valley, gathering nearly forty fighters for a final assault.

They hid in the cellar near the pillar until sunrise, then charged into the dungeon. Reapers attacked from all sides, targeting torchbearers to extinguish the only thing that weakened them. Serqet’s cart‑light carved a path through the darkness, scattering the creatures long enough for the group to reach the pit.

Perry lifted the cart and shone its beam down the shaft, halting the Reapers’ ascent. Ahgythah and Jack combined their strength to collapse the ceiling above, sealing the pit temporarily.

Ton’s explosive detonated moments later. Fire erupted from every opening of the pillar before the entire structure collapsed, taking much of the Crumbling City with it. Twenty warriors died, and the survivors were forever changed—but the Reapers were buried.

For now.
