AOR 542-5-17

After resting a developing a new attack strategy, the party return to the pincers’ cave, sticking closer together and using ranged attacks whenever possible to keep the Sect at bay. This attempt was much more successful, quickly cutting down their opposition. They may have reduced the creatures’ numbers more than intended, but they had still technically accomplished their mission.

The party returned to the Echoing Isle and reported to the Secret Quill. This gained them some trust and new information. Though they were not willing to divulge all of their secrets, they Quill did share with the party that the survivors of Pallium had been gathering resources throughout the Sunless Sea. They did not know for what purpose, but it had resulted in the fuel & food shortages experienced in the Isle of Lights.

AOR 542-5-18 to 19

Unsure what to do with this new information, the party tended to their wounds and rested, preparing for what lay ahead.
