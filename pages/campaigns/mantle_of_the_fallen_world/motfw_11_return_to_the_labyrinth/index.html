Summary

The party agreed to escort Cas Twyn and a contingent of Dryn back to Sanctum. After a day of preparation, they traveled through the valley and re‑entered the Crumbling Labyrinth. Strange vibrations and distant movement hinted that something was stalking them.

At night, their camp was attacked by a swarm of Pincers. The Dryn, though well‑trained, were inexperienced in real combat and suffered heavy injuries. Only Ahgythah’s healing prevented fatalities. Rasat arrived shortly after, drawn by the blue flare Ahgythah had lit.

Rasat revealed that the Last Hope had detonated an explosive inside the pillar, but it had only cracked the structure—draining the city’s water and disturbing deeper Sect nests. Worse, a hidden dungeon beneath the pillar had been exposed, and the Hermit had vanished into its depths.

The party continued to Sanctum, where they delivered Cas Twyn and the Dryn escort safely. That night, Athyna received a terrifying vision from the Hermit: a bottomless pit beneath the pillar, claw marks on the walls, and a warning that something ancient and deadly was rising from the depths.

The message was clear—the pillar had to be destroyed completely, or the Labyrinth would fall.
