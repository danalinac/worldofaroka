MOTFW 17. THE SUNLESS SEA

Summary

Banished from the valley, the party retreated into the caverns. After days of rest, they reached the underground sea—a vast black expanse illuminated only by a distant artificial sun atop a tower.

Following the shoreline, they discovered a lone Honeybadger‑Kin warming himself by a fire. He introduced himself as Tyche, a former resident of Pallium who had been stranded after angering too many ship crews. He offered to guide the party to Pallium if they helped him reclaim a run‑aground boat guarded by giant crabs.

The party agreed. After sharing food and stories, they slept—though Ahgythah and Tyche slipped away for some very audible “private time,” much to the party’s dismay.

The next morning, they located the grounded vessel and slew the giant crabs inhabiting it. Before they could celebrate, a shoal‑shark lunged from the water, drawn by the scent of blood. The fight was vicious, but the party prevailed.

With the boat repaired and fresh rations carved from their kills, the party prepared to set sail across the Sunless Sea—toward the Isle of Lights, and eventually, Pallium.

The next stage of their journey had begun.
