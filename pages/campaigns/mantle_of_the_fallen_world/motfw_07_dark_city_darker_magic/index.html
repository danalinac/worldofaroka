AOR 542-4-64 to 65

Recovering from the Weaver battle, the party waited an additional three days while Rasat scouted the next region of the Crumbling Labyrinth.

AOR 542-4-66

Rasat reported that this next part of the Labyrinth consisted of a ruined subterranean city dominated by a massive central pillar. The recent quakes had collapsed bridges many of its bridges and opened new crevasses, but he believed he had identified a path to the only known exit from the Labyrinth this section. He also informed the party that the area seemed devoid of all life, even the aquatic creatures and Sect that had previously dominated this region.

He gave the party a colored flare Ton had created that would be easy for his to spot while flying overhead. If they were in distress or otherwise wanted to get a hold of him, they could light this and he would be there as quickly as possible.

AOR 542-4-67

On the second day of navigating the Crumbling City, the party discovered a trail of corpses—one of which had seemed to stand a walk away according to the tracks. Soon after, Perry encountered a Mimic, a rare tentacled creature capable of effectively taking nearly any form a color necessary to blend into its environment and whose toxin was known to numb the minds of its victims, making them easier to lure into a trap. Despite Ahgythah’s warnings, Perry picked up a suspiciously pretty rock and was promptly poisoned the Mimic. While they were able to chase the Mimic off before it could do any more harm, Perry would still be under the influence of the toxic for several hours, forcing the party to seek shelter for the night early.

AOR 542-4-68

The next morning, a swarm of Mosquito-Folk attacked. After a difficult fight, the survivors opponents were incinerated by a sudden burst of magical flame. A hooded figure approached, introducing itself to the party as the Hermit, and that it meant them no harm as long as they meant it no harm.

The Hermit revealed that a Sect hive within the central pillar was practicing dark magic, reanimating corpses into Husks to fuel their rituals. It offered knowledge in exchange for the party’s help in destroying the hive. The party lit their flair and summoned Rasat. He was skeptical, but agreed to pass on the message to the Last Hope and see if they would join in the siege of the pillar.

AOR 542-4-69

Rasat returned the following morning to inform the party that the Last Hope would aid them in clearing the Pillar of sect in hopes that it they could use it as a new home if it proved more defensible than Sanctum. The Last Hope would need the day to prepare and would launch their attack the following dawn.

The party spent the rest of the day scouting the Pillar and the surrounding area, searching for ways to sneak in while the Mosquito-Folk were distracted by the Last Hope’s attack.
