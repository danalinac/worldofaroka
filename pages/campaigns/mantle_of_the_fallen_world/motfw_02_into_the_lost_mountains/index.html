AOR 542-4-42 to 43

The party departed Kendathyl Warf with a company of two dozen other adventurers, each group wary of the others as they began the long trek along the coast. Strange figures stalked their camp at night, and tensions rose as the path grew colder and more treacherous.

AOR 542-4-44

Around midday, the company came across a group of travelers that had gotten their wagon stuck in the mud. Without anything with which to reward anyone that helped them, the travelers asked for the kindness of the adventurers to free their wagon from the mud so they could continue their journey to Kendathyl Warf. One of the groups of the company decided to stop and help while the rest, including the party, continued on their way.

AOR 542-4-45

The company reached a destroyed village—many of its inhabitants slain by blade, claw, and magic. The locals that had remained we unwilling to speak to the newcomers and the traders that had set up shop amongst the ruins didn’t know what had befallen the village. With night quickly approaching, the party searched for a suitable place to spend the night without having to contend with whomever had been stalking them the previous nights.

While searching for shelter, the party discovered a hidden cellar containing supplies and a long‑dead occupant. That night, they heard armored pursuers searched the ruins above them, confirming that someone was hunting them.

AOR 542-4-46

The following morning, the rest of the company had gotten a head start, leaving the party behind to deal with whomever was stalking them. Forced to continue their journey alone, the party ascended into the foothills. If the steeper terrain and colder air were not difficult enough, the weather also became overcast and threatened to rain.

A nighttime ambush by three assassins revealed that their enemies were both skilled and determined. The party survived, but the attackers’ focus on Jack made their motives clear. While confident that the immediate danger had been dealt with, anyone with the knowledge of what Jack possessed and the resources to send skilled assassins after him would likely try again and with greater numbers.

AOR 542-4-47

Further up the mountain, they encountered a lone grave beneath a tree and unearthed valuable equipment—though Serqet unknowingly contracted a disease from the corpse. Later, they reached a merchant encampment blocking a mountain pass. Trading supplies for shelter, they rested before continuing toward the Frozen Cliffs.

AOR 542-4-48

Upon reaching the cliffs, the party caught up with the rest of the company that had left them behind. Many were already quite a ways up the icy face. As the party began to join them in the climb, a violent quake shook the earth, bringing down much of the cliff and killing many of the other adventurers. When the dust and snow settled, the entrances to an ancient underground structure was revealed partway up the cliff face. Seeking refuge from the devastation, the party descended into the newly exposed ruins—unaware of the dangers waiting in the depths.
