AOR 542-4-50

After finally finding their way out of the maze, the party reached a vast circular clearing dominated by a towering structure lit with multicolored lights. A stone slab warned travelers to wait or die. Heeding the message, the party stood still until three individuals emerged from the tower. They introduced themselves as Arxst (a Rhino-Kin), Atalos (a Fox-Kin), and Naloi (A Dryn-Goat Bryd) and represented the Last Hope.

The Last Hope were adventures that had been trapped in the Labyrinth for years, having carved out a home in the tower they now called Sanctum. They offered the party shelter and aid in exchange for their service. Inside Sanctum, the party learned the history of the Last Hope, their tenuous alliance with a mysterious company, called the Var’Yn, that had recently traveled through the Labyrinth, and the dangers that had reshaped the Labyrinth after recent quakes.

They also met Rasat, a Bat-Kin scout of the Last Hope, and Yjune, a Cat-Kin mage specializing in detecting deceit and threats.

AOR 542-4-51

To earn the Last Hope’s trust, the party agreed to clear the Well—an essential water source now infested with dangerous creatures after the most recent quake. They slew a massive tentacled beast and several smaller predators, returning with enough meat to feed Sanctum for days and restored access to their water source. Their success inspired many warriors to join them in carving a path forward through the Labyrinth.

AOR 542-4-52

With momentum on their side, the party led a strike against a Beetle‑Folk colony blocking the path deeper into the Labyrinth. The battle was fierce, and though many warriors fell, the party’s tactics secured victory at the cost of collapsing a passage that both prevent enemy reinforcements and passage to the next section of the Labyrinth. Ton, a Dryn tinkerer with an unhealthy obsession with mixing chemicals together to see what happened, proposed crafting an explosive to clear the blockage—setting the stage for the next phase of their journey.
