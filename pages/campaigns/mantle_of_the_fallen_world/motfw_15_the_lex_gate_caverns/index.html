Summary

With the pillar destroyed and the Reapers buried, the party returned to Sanctum to recover. The Crumbling City continued to collapse around the former pillar site, making the region increasingly unstable. Most of the Last Hope chose to abandon the Labyrinth entirely.

The party agreed to escort Cas Twyn, Ton, and twenty Last Hope refugees back to Lex‑Gate Valley. Along the way, they encountered a group of traveling merchants hoping to establish trade with Sanctum. Learning of its collapse, they instead joined the caravan in exchange for a mysterious promised reward.

The journey through the Crumble was perilous. Collapsing ceilings, widening chasms, and unstable ground claimed several lives. After days of navigating the dying Labyrinth, the caravan finally emerged into the valley.

The Dryn welcomed their returning kin, though the Anthropes were required to camp outside the main settlement. The merchants honored their promise, gifting the party a magical pendant capable of storing energy.

With their contract fulfilled, the party now faced new choices:
– integrate the Last Hope refugees into the valley,
– investigate the mysteries of Lex and the Gate,
– or continue their pursuit of Pallium using the newly acquired cavern map.

For the first time in a long while, they had room to breathe—and to decide their future.
