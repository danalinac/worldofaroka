MOTFW 25. THE LESSER EVIL

Summary

To honor their agreement with the Var’Yn, the party ambushed the Guide as he returned to retrieve them. They hurled rubble onto his vessel, sinking it beneath the cavern waters. As a robotic Pallian host, he could not drown—but he was delayed long enough for the Var’Yn to extract the party.

A winged Var’Yn carried them through a high tunnel and out into daylight. Before them stretched a vast valley: forests, mountains, a collapsing volcano to the east, and—most striking of all—the metallic spires of Pallium rising to the west.

At the Var’Yn’s volcanic base, the party met Kal’Ah, commander of the expedition. He revealed the truth of the Var’Yn: descendants of a void‑faring people who once sought to preserve life across worlds, defeated during the War of Ashes by unknown forces wielding comparable technologies. Those forces, he warned, were stirring again.

To oppose them, the Var’Yn needed Pallium’s ancient machines.

The party, wary of the Pallians’ plan to convert all organic life into robotic hosts, chose the Var’Yn as the “lesser evil.”

At dawn, they mounted winged Var’Yn and flew toward Pallium. The volcano erupted outward, scorching a path through the valley—fuel for the Var’Yn foot soldiers, who absorbed energy from the burning land. The scale of destruction gave the party pause, but it was too late to turn back.

Inside Pallium, they reached the chamber of the city’s secret weapon: a massive robotic beast wired to awaken. Pallian engineers raised the alarm, and battle erupted.

Sensing the arrival of the Slayer—the unstoppable executioner sent by the Knights of the Scroll—Jack made a desperate choice. He released the god within the Decanter, allowing it to possess the robotic titan. The machine roared to life, fueled by the souls Jack had harvested.

Perry, enraged by the betrayal, beat Jack nearly to death. The god finished the job, consuming Jack’s body and spirit in a final act of “gratitude.”

The Slayer arrived, an ancient armored husk of magic and machinery, but even it could not stop the newborn god. The party chose the third path: let the god go free, denying both Pallium and the Var’Yn their prize.

What followed is uncertain. Legends speak of:

  • deranged Var’Yn,
  • rogue Pallian constructs,
  • and a wandering god‑beast
    stalking the valley.

The party, weary of war, is believed to have settled among a Dryn village in the unburnt forests—choosing peace over destiny.

The fate of Pallium, the Var’Yn, and the god remains unknown.
