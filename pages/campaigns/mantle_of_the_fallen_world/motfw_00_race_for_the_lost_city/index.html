Race for the Lost City

Mantle of the Fallen World Session 0

Dates
In‑World:AOR 542‑4‑39
Real‑World:2024‑08‑25

Summary

Since the dawn of the Age of Ice, the legends of Pallium—the Mantle of the Fallen World—have endured. Said to be the birthplace of terrible weapons forged during the War of Ashes, the Lost City remained unreachable for centuries, buried deep within the perilous Lost Mountains. But as the 541st year of the Age of Reclamation ended, rumors spread of a new, safer route into the mountains. When the Three Suns returned after the Dark Days, the race for Pallium began.

Jack, a Hare‑Kin adventurer from Ohedyn, had spent the months leading up to the Dark Days exploring ruins in the Sandsea of Duva with his hired local Scorpion-Kin guide, Serqet. Only the two of them survived the expedition, and Jack carried with him a strange, glowing decanter of unknown origin. From the Sandsea they traveled across Morydal, over the Tryvyum Sea, and into Amyhra—where they learned of the race for Pallium and immediately set out.

Their journey north through the Shattered Strait brought them to Westguard, only to find it destroyed and haunted by Gaunt. A lone survivor, Perry the Platypus‑Kin warrior, saved them from a massive Gaunt attack and joined their quest, hoping Pallium held weapons capable of destroying the creatures once and for all.

The group continued north, eventually reaching Kendathyl Warf on the 39th day of the fourth month of AOR 542. There, they prepared for the final push into the Lost Mountains—toward the Mantle of the Fallen World and whatever dangers awaited within.
