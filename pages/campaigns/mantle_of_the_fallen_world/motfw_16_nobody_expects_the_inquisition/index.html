AOR 542-5-01

After brief deliberation, the party elected to return to Lex-Gate Valley to bring back the news of the passing of the two Seekers. They encountered no more difficulties and made it back to the valley just before sunset.

AOR 542-5-02

Appreciative of the party’s service to their community, the Lex-Gate Elders provided them supplies that would aide them in the next stage of their quest and informed the party that they could remain in the valley as long as they desired.

AOR 542-5-03

Around mid morning, the party was informed that there was a company of warriors that had made camp on the shore of the nearby lake. A messenger was sent to them and they requested an audience with Jack. Unsure of who these newcomers were and what they wanted with Jack, so the party made their way to the gate. Though it was difficult to tell at that distance, it seemed as if the newcomers were well armed. No banners were flown and color patterns or Other identifying elements. Cautiously, the party exited the gate and walked until they were halfway between the gate and the newcomers.

A lone Bird-Kin in armor marked with two crossed swords and a scroll, indicating him to be a member of the Knights of Scroll, namely their Inquisitor division, approached the party. He informed the party that he wished to speak with Jack alone. It took some convincing, but Jack agreed to walk a short distance from the rest of the party to speak with him. He informed Jack that The Decanter he possessed was extremely dangerous and that he should hand it over to the charge of the Knights of the Scroll. Jack, having formed a relationship with the entity living within The Decanter, obviously refused. In response, the Inquisitor began to unleash and magical attack against Jack. However, Jack was ready and struck first with Magic fueled by The Decanter. Spectral tentacles wrapped around the Inquisitor, immobilizing him. This bought Jack and his party enough time to dispatch the Inquisitor before the rest of his companions could charge them.

The battle that followed was challenging as the party’s opponents were well-equipped, well-trained, and some possessed magical capabilities. Not all seemed to be members of the Knights of the Scroll, but were instead highly-skilled mercenaries. When the dust settled, most of the party members were severally wounded and all but two of the enemy remained alive. One, another Bird-Kin, was able to flee and the other, a Tortoise-Kin, was taken captive.
