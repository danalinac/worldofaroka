Battle of Blood

Mantle of the Fallen World Session 9

Real‑World:2025‑02‑02
Participants
Factions:The Hive, The Last Hope
NPCs:Ahedyn Girl, Hermit, Last Hope Warriors
PCs:Ahedyn Girl (Unnamed), Ahgythah, Helianth, Jack, Perry
Locations
Continent:Roa

Territory:Crumbling Labyrinth
Areas:Crumbling City
Points:The Pillar
AOR 542-4-70

Ready for whatever lay ahead, the Hermit, the Last Hope, and the party ascended the stairs and began to push open the large metal doors. They were heavy and there was clearly debris stacked against it on the other side, but they slowly began to push it ajar. Once a large enough gap had been created, Ahgythah squeezed through to clear away the rubble and pull one of the doors open. She was instantly bombarded with a shower of arrows and stones, but her armor shielded her from harm long enough for her to pull open the door and let her allies in.

The throne room was a vast, dark space with blood-stained floors and grotesque piles of deteriorating flesh pressed up against its walls and pillars. A small army of Mosquito-Folk stood between them and what appeared to be the rulers of the hive. Several large and monstrous Mosquito-Folk stood at the far end of the hall, some of which had been augmented with additional appendages, limbs, and other abominable features. Beside them stood a tall, lizard like being with two sets of arms and multiple heads magically grafted to its body. Even those not attuned to magic could sense the power emanating from this being.

The battle was fierce. These Mosquito-Folk proved more formidable than the ones the party previous faced and they were supported by mighty Husks—reanimated corpses of large Sect—that fought with abandon. When the first line of Mosquito-Folk had been defeated, the dark mage began to animate the blood and rotting flesh scattered throughout the throne room, bringing forth masses of gore and viscera to attack and terrify the Last Hope.

During the battle, several members of the Last Hope proved themselves to be quite formidable, fighting alongside the party as they tore through waves of enemies. Je’Olt, an Axolotl-Kin, charged ahead with Perry, defending him against the shorter enemies while he took down the larger ones. Jorge, a Hyena-Kin, stood his ground against many enemies far larger than him, even falling several times and getting back up to fight again with a little help from Ahgythah’s healing. Helianth, a Cat-Horse-Bryd, and Augustus, an Otter-Kin, fought their way along the wall to try and sneak past the main defense and strike the dark mage and the hive rulers. While unsuccessful in reaching their targets, they did manage to fend off some particular aggressive Husks without taking any injuries themselves. Additionally, the Ahedyn girl, fueled by her hatred for the Mosquito-Folk, unleashed powerful blood conduit magic on all that dared attack her, cutting down even the largest Husks with ease.

As the Mosquito-Folks’ numbers diminished, the dark mage attempted to escape, but was caught and quickly subdued by the party and their allies. His offensive magic made him a dangerous adversary and even his defensive magic made him nearly impervious to ranged attacks, but once he was within melee range, he succumbed quickly to the combined might of the Last Hope and the party. The rulers of the hive put up a good final fight, but they too fell. Thanks to the efforts of Ahgythah and the other healers, only six warriors of the Last Hope perished, though many were so injured that they would likely be unable to fight ever again.

Disgusted by the abominations that had been created within the pillar, the Last Hope began to formulate a plan to craft another explosive to bring down the pillar and bury all the evil created within beneath its rubble. The Hermit, not believing that would be enough, asked for time to search the pillar and cleanse it of evil before they brought it down. The Last Hope agreed and made preparations to return to Sanctum to treat their wounds and assess how many battle-ready warriors they still possessed. The party agreed to return to Sanctum as well to recover before continuing on their journey.
