AOR 542-5-03

The party took their Tortoise-Kin captive to a nearby cave and interrogated him. They learned that he was a mercenary that had worked for the Knights of the Scroll for some time, recovering ancient artifacts and eliminating those with dangerous knowledge. He urged the party to surrender The Decanter as it was far more dangerous than they could imagine. If they refused, he informed them that the Knights of the Scroll would send a larger force, but not before they sent a Slayer to eliminate the party, secure The Decanter, and deliver it to them upon their arrival. Jack, angered that an entire organization was after him and would send a legendary Slayer after him, used the Magic of The Decanter to extract their captive’s soul, killing him. The party, believing they could have gotten more information out of him, proceeded to attack Jack, dealing a number of non-lethal injuries in their frustration.

Once the rest of the party had beaten up Jack enough to subside their frustration, returned to Lex-Gate Valley. The Elders have already been summoned and they were greatly concerned with the conflict and use of Magic just outside their gates. In response, they were now having all the Anthropes and Dryn from Sanctum would reside outside the gates until another arrangement could be negotiated. They also required the party to leave the valley and never return. While not ideal, the party didn’t want to cause any more problems for the survivors from Sanctum, so they agreed. They packed up their gear and spent the night in the entrance of the Caverns.

AOR 542-5-04 to 06

During their first day in the caverns, the party traveled to the room where they had found the glowing pipes. They spent the rest of that day and the following two days recovering from their fight the previous day.

AOR 542-5-07

After fully recovering, the party made their way through the caverns to the passage that led to the underground sea. In the distance, a bright light atop a tower shown across the region, illuminating the Sea of Light. Many of the islands had lights flickering on them and several vessels could be spotted traveling between them.

Unsure of the path forward, the party made their way along the clifftops looking for a way down to the shore. They eventually did and soon found themselves walking along a dark and frigid beach. After traveling along it for a couple hours, they spotted a small fire at the base of a cliff. Cautiously, they approached, identifying a lone Honeybadger-Kin warming himself by the fire. They called out to him, stating that they did not wish to fight. The stranger was wary, but allowed the party to approach. He introduced himself as Tyche and inquired as to how the party had found their way into the Sunless Sea and why. They informed him that they were seeking the Lost City of Pallium. Intrigued, Tyche informed them that he had lived in Pallium prior to the First Firefall and could guide them to it. He had spotted a run-aground boat on the beach nearby but was unable to claim it as some giant crabs had made it their home. If the party served as his muscle, we would help them reach Pallium and beyond.

With an uneasy alliance established, the party shared their food with Tyche. Shortly after a horn sounded from the main island of the region, the bright light went out, plunging them into darkness. Huddling close to each Other and their campfire, they drifted off to sleep in preparation for the next leg of their adventure.

During the night, Ahgythah and Tyche, being the two oldest members of the group, took a liking to each Other and snuck off from the party. Despite attempting to get far enough away to not disturb the rest of their party, their sounds echoed against the cliffs, disturbing the sleep and souls of the rest of their party.

AOR 542-5-08

The following morning, after The Light had turned back on, they set out to the grounded vessel. They quickly found it and dispatched the giant crabs that had made a home out of it. However, before they could claim the vessel and begin making repairs, a shoal-shark emerged from the waters having been drawn by the blood from the crabs. The creature struck hard, injuring some of the party members, but their combined attacks were able to bring it down. Once sure that they would be no more surprises, the party divided their efforts with half of them making repairs to the boat and the rest carving up the crabs and shoal-shark for rations.
