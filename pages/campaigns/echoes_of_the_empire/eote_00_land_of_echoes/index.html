EOTE 00. LAND OF ECHOES

Dates
In‑World:AOR 542-5-76
Real‑World:2026-01-11

The players and the Scribe gathered to establish the foundation of the campaign world. Using a bag of dice rolled across a whiteboard, the group collaboratively generated the geography of the Eastern Echoes — forests, mountains, rivers, settlements, and remnants of past civilizations. This exercise set the tone for the campaign’s frontier exploration and emergent storytelling.

The Scribe further flushed out the region and campaigns, detailing the territories of the Ashlands, Burning Sea, Dustveil, Hallowed Crest, Savage East, and Wild West and the factions of Ember Guard, Fellowship of the Free Folk, and the Scyonic Imperium.
